Biography

Hello! I am currently a third-year Ph.D. student in Bioengineering at the University of Washington, mentored by Dr. Azadeh Yazdan-Shahmorad. My research focuses on refining approaches to engineer neuroplasticity for translational neurotechnology applications.

Combining electrophysiology, neuromodulation, and computational modeling, I aim to better understand cortical plasticity in primates and advance the design of targeted neuromodulation strategies.
