Biography

Hello! I am a third-year Ph.D. student in the Department of Bioengineering at the University of Washington, mentored by Dr. Azadeh Yazdan-Shahmorad. My research focuses on refining approaches to engineer neuroplasticity for translational neurotechnology applications.

Combining electrophysiology, neuromodulation, and computational modeling, I aim to better understand cortical plasticity in primates and advance the design of targeted neuromodulation strategies.

Projects

Neurovascular Unit Instability during Ischemic Stroke

Studying the neural and neurovascular unit response due to ischemic stroke induced using the photothrombotic lesion technique in NHP.

Inferring Regional Cortical Plasticity using Correlation-based Prediction Model

A correlation-based functional connectivity prediction model inspired by Kempter's theorem to infer regional-specific cortical plasticity.

Encephalophone: a music BCI

A musical brain-computer interface using motor cortex EEG features to produce intentional and real-time music tone production.
